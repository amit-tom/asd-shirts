FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Everything about the fabric, the fit, and the commission — no showroom visit required.

What makes Suvin cotton so rare?
Suvin represents less than one kilo out of every 38 tons of cotton grown globally and is cultivated exclusively in India. It features an ultra-fine 2.9 micronaire count (finer than Egyptian Giza), which yields a natural lustre and fluid drape without synthetic coatings or chemical treatments.
Do you offer other colors or fabrics?
No. We believe choice after a certain point becomes clutter. We dedicate our entire craft to a single material and color: rare white Suvin cotton.
How does the fabric hold up over time?
Because the staple fibers are exceptionally long and resilient, the fabric softens with every wash while retaining its crisp weave structure and tensile strength.
How should I care for my shirt?
Machine wash on a gentle, cold cycle or hand wash. Hang dry in shade. Iron warm or steam lightly. Avoid harsh bleaching agents to preserve the natural lustre of the Suvin fibers.
How does made-to-measure sizing work online?
You can either input your body measurements through our guided tool, send us the dimensions of your best-fitting shirt, or request a master tailor consultation call.
Can I customize the collar and cuff styles?
Yes. You can select one of our curated lookbook styles or configure your own collar spread, cuff type, placket, and monogramming at no additional cost.
What if the fit isn't right on my first order?
We stand by our standard. If your initial shirt requires adjustments, we offer a complimentary re-fitting and alteration within 14 days of delivery. We then update your master profile for all future commissions.
Why is your annual production limited?
True Suvin harvest volumes are naturally finite, and our single-needle bench tailoring takes deliberate time. We cap our commissions to ensure we never compromise on construction or stitch density.
What is the delivery timeline?
Because every shirt is individually cut and stitched to order, crafting takes 10–14 business days, followed by 2–4 days for insured domestic shipping.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes. We ship worldwide via express logistics. International delivery typically takes 5–7 business days after crafting is complete.
What is your return policy?
Since every shirt is custom-drafted to individual measurements, we do not accept standard returns. However, our Perfect Fit Guarantee covers any necessary alterations or remakes until the garment meets your exact standard.
